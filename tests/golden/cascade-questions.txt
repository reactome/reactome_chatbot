# --- facts ABOUT the database, which retrieval structurally cannot answer ----
# Measured 2026-09-14 on beta. "What species are included in the Reactome
# database?" routes to `reactome` correctly (5/5), retrieves 40 documents, and
# is answered:
#
#   "The Reactome Knowledgebase primarily focuses on Homo sapiens (humans).
#    There are no indications of other species being included."
#
# Reactome covers 96 species. The MCP's reactome_species returns all of them
# with taxonomy IDs.
#
# Nothing is broken here: the corpus is a sample of pathway content, and those
# 40 documents really are human. A question about the database's SCOPE cannot
# be answered from a sample of its CONTENT, however good the retrieval gets.
# These need the API, not a better index.
